Baad Mein

Everyone has a WhatsApp chat with themselves. Links, “dahi bread aur maggi”, “Raju ko 500 dena”, an OTP from March. Things you'll deal with later.

Gemma reads that chat slowly, on your own device, and gives you one card in the morning: what's due, what to buy, who owes what, and what you can finally let go.

How to get your chat out of WhatsApp
  1. Open the chat with yourself (it has your name and says “(You)”).
  2. Tap ⋮ → More → Export chat → Without media. On iPhone, tap your name → Export Chat.
  3. Save the file, then choose it with Read my chat. A .zip from iPhone works too.
  • Nothing leaves this device. Gemma 4 runs inside this tab. No account, no server, no API key.
  • OTPs and card numbers are never shown to the model. Plain code finds them first and puts them on a “delete these” list.
  • Slow is fine. Leave it running overnight. If the tab closes, it picks up where it stopped.
